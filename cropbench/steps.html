How the model played

Every tool call an agent made, as decision steps in fixed classes so models can be compared by eye. Derived from the exported traces, ledgers and transcript tool calls.

Deviation from a reference trajectory

Agent 1 in colour over the reference in grey. + taken but reference did not · − reference did, agent skipped · Δ same step, different amount or timing · forks = different crop until the two converge. Click a node to open the replay on that day.

Swimlane timeline

x = simulated date. One lane per step class; behind them a band per field (crop colour, darker as the stage advances) and blue rain ticks. Stack agents to compare cadence.

Decision DAG

Nodes = decision stops by date; within a stop the steps form a column ordered by class (sense on top, control at the bottom) in call order. Edges between stops are as long as the advance. Hover for arguments and results; click to open the replay on that day.

Strategy fingerprints

Step classes on a fixed ring; edge thickness = how often one class follows another; node size = call count. Small multiples in leaderboard order, with the crop sequence per field below for rotation scenarios.
Steps come from the verified transcript tool calls when the exported attempt has them (⚙ in the picker), otherwise from the trace and ledger (field actions, soil tests, harvests, rejected calls; sensing and planning calls are then unknown and stops are inferred weekly).
