Hey! I'm Jared


I'm a Senior Engineer on NVIDIA's BioNeMo team. Previously, I co-founded a company (YC S23) and was an Applied Scientist at AWS.

My work/research is focused on machine learning and data visualization. After going through open-heart surgery in my mid-twenties, I pivoted that work towards biology.

I live with my fiancé and pug in San Diego, California 🌴. Outside of work I like to surf, skateboard, and practice yoga (still can't sit cross-legged, though).
